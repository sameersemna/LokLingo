# Governance Operating Calendar

Governance Version: 1.0
Review Owner: Reliability Lead (Platform)
Last Review: 2026-05-12
Next Review: 2026-08-12
Operational Scope: Weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual governance operating rhythm

This calendar defines the minimum governance rhythm required to keep reliability operations current without adding unnecessary ceremony.

## Weekly Rhythm

| Activity | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reliability KPI review | Service owners + platform | Current risks, breaches, action updates |
| Smoke-test health review | QA automation + service owners | Flake fixes, failing flows, provider drift |
| Incident follow-up review | Incident owners | Updated action tracker state |
| Dashboard noise review | Observability owner | Alert tuning candidates |

## Monthly Rhythm

| Activity | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance signoff | Service owner group | Completed monthly signoff template |
| Threshold drift review | Service owners + SRE/platform | Approved threshold changes or no-change note |
| Template usability review | Incident commander rotation | Friction notes and template improvements |
| Change log update sweep | Reliability lead | Governance change log current for the month |

## Quarterly Rhythm

| Activity | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance review | Reliability lead | Completed quarterly review packet |
| SLO/SLA recalibration | Service owners | Updated objectives if needed |
| Lifecycle event coverage review | Backend platform owner | Instrumentation gaps and actions |
| Governance archive hygiene | Reliability lead | Archived review artifacts and index confirmation |

## Annual Rhythm

| Activity | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual governance summary | Reliability lead | Leadership-ready annual summary |
| Maturity roadmap refresh | Platform + product leadership | Next-year operating priorities |
| Policy simplification pass | Service owners | Removed outdated/unused governance overhead |

## Scheduling Rules

- Weekly reviews should occur on the same weekday whenever possible.
- Monthly signoff should complete within the first 5 business days of the next month.
- Quarterly review should complete within 10 business days of quarter close.
- Annual summary should complete before yearly planning closes.
